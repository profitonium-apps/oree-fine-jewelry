    Before you decide

    Questions, answered.

    Still deciding? A jeweller will answer by video, phone or in the salon, whichever feels easiest.

    Are lab-grown diamonds real diamonds?

    Yes. They are carbon crystallised exactly like a mined diamond, with the same hardness, fire and brilliance. Only the origin differs, and every stone over 0.3 ct comes with an independent IGI report.

    I don't know the ring size. What now?

    Order a free ring sizer, use our printable guide, or simply pick your best guess: resizing is free within 60 days of delivery.

    How long does delivery take?

    Pieces in stock ship in 3–5 business days, made-to-order pieces in 7–10. Delivery is free, insured and signed for across the US & Canada.

    Can I return it?

    Within 30 days, unworn, for a full refund with a prepaid label. Engraved and bespoke pieces are final sale.

    Do you engrave?

    On rings, signets and medallions, up to 20 characters, complimentary. Add your text on the product page.

    How do I care for solid gold?

    Warm water, a drop of mild soap, a soft brush. Send it back to us any time for a free professional clean and claw check.
