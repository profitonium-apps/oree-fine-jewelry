    De la récolte au packshot

    From harvest to packshot, in six chapters.

    I

    La Récolte · Harvest

    It starts with gold that already exists.

    Old jewelry, workshop filings and broken chains are gathered, assayed and refined back to pure gold before we alloy it to 18k. Nothing is newly mined.

    0 g newly mined gold

    II

    La Fonte · Melt

    Poured by hand at 1,064 °C.

    We melt in small batches so every ingot can be traced to the pieces it becomes. The pour takes seconds; the alloy is checked before and after.

    1,064 °C where gold turns liquid

    III

    La Forge · Form

    Drawn, rolled, rounded.

    The ingot is rolled flat, drawn through steel plates until it is fine as a band, then rounded on the mandrel and soldered closed. Forged metal is denser, so it wears longer.

    18 passes through the drawplate

    IV

    Le Sertissage · Set

    Four claws, one steady hand.

    The stone is seated, then each claw is pushed over by hand and checked under the loupe. Low enough for knitwear, tight enough for a lifetime.

    0.1 mm tolerance on every claw

    V

    Le Polissage · Polish

    Three polishes, two inspections.

    Felt, then cotton, then a final mirror polish. A second jeweller inspects the piece before it is hallmarked. If it isn't right, it goes back to the bench.

    2 sets of eyes on every piece

    VI

    Le Packshot · Photograph

    You see your piece before we send it.

    Every order is photographed in our studio in natural light, and the picture is emailed to you before dispatch. Then it is boxed in olive leather, tied in honey ribbon and insured to your door.

    1 photo of your actual piece

      Before you decide

      Questions, answered.

      Still deciding? A jeweller will answer by video, phone or in the atelier, whichever feels easiest.

      Talk to a jeweller
      Are lab-grown diamonds real diamonds?

      Yes. They are carbon crystallised exactly like a mined diamond, with the same hardness, fire and brilliance. Only the origin differs, and every stone over 0.3 ct comes with an independent grading report.

      How does 0.0% APR work?

      At checkout you can split the price into four equal instalments, paid every two weeks, with no interest and no fees when each is paid on time. Approval depends on eligibility; the full note is at the foot of this page.

      I don't know the ring size. What now?

      Measure a ring she already wears with our on-screen sizer, or pick your best guess: resizing is free within 60 days of delivery.

      How long does delivery take?

      Pieces in stock ship in 3–5 business days, made-to-order pieces in 7–10. You receive a photograph of your piece before it leaves the atelier.

      Can I return it?

      Within 30 days, unworn, for a full refund with a prepaid label. Engraved and bespoke pieces are final sale.

      Do you engrave?

      Inside rings and on the back of medallions, up to 20 characters, at no charge. You'll see a preview as you type.

      Letters from the atelier.

      New pieces a week early, the odd photograph from the bench, and a care kit with your first order.
